# Future Levels — Reserved Extension Points

Level 1 deliberately establishes schema and interfaces for later escalation without implementing later content.

Reserved, not implemented in Level 1:

- Flame Levels 2+ and corresponding offering recipes.
- Passage through Deadly Shroud after the required Flame level is earned.
- Multiple Deadly-Shroud tiers or additional colors/severities.
- Secondary/mobile Shroud nodes spawned by mature cores.
- Cross-dimensional Shroud propagation.
- Additional Lich Manifestations with new spell schools, phases, summons and arena rules.
- Discovery/destruction of the true phylactery.
- World-scale corruption events or raids.
- Dedicated Shroud dimension.
- Advanced equipment that extends exposure time or grants specialized passage resistance.

Schema rule: serialized enums are stored by stable string IDs or numeric schema fields with codecs; future additions must not reorder ordinal-backed persistence.

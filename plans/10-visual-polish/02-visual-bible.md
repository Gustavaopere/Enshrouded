# Stage 10 — Visual Bible

## Core identity

Enshrouded visual design combines four pillars:

1. **Ancient Flame** — protection, order, warmth, progression.
2. **Living Shroud** — invasive, organic, cold, unstable corruption.
3. **Ruined ritual architecture** — old civilization, monumental stone/metal frames, intentional geometry.
4. **Lich manifestation** — death, bone, fractured ritual geometry and spectral instability.

The mod must not look like a random collection of fantasy assets. Every new asset must map to one or more of these pillars.

## Shape language

### Flame / Sanctuary
- vertical central axis;
- radial symmetry;
- stable rings, arches, triangles and stepped framing;
- carved channels/runes pointing toward a protected center;
- deliberate negative space;
- slow controlled animation rhythm.

### Ordinary Shroud
- branching curves;
- veins and roots;
- asymmetric clusters;
- membranes, ribs and swollen nodules;
- slow organic pulse;
- silhouettes that look grown rather than built.

### Deadly Shroud
- same family, more hostile geometry;
- sharper splits/thorns;
- exposed internal energy;
- heavier, darker mass;
- stronger contrast and faster pulse;
- distinguishable from Ordinary even in grayscale.

### Lich
- bone/mask structure;
- broken halo/crown motifs;
- floating fragments;
- runic gaps and damaged symmetry;
- spectral separation between solid and immaterial pieces.

## Palette

### Flame
- charcoal / blackened stone;
- aged stone gray;
- dark brass / old metal;
- deep ember orange;
- amber and restrained gold;
- warm white only at hottest focal points.

### Ordinary Shroud
- blue-gray;
- desaturated violet;
- cold pale highlights;
- sickly green accent only in small amounts.

### Deadly Shroud
- dark crimson;
- oxidized blood red;
- deep magenta;
- near-black organic material;
- bright internal hostile red.

### Sanctuary / Purification
- visual relation to Flame through warm gold;
- pale clean cyan/white relief accent;
- cleaner surfaces and upward motion;
- no generic saturated-blue “magic bubble” look.

## Material language

Hero assets should visibly separate materials:
- stone is matte, heavy and chipped;
- metal is structured and used as frame/brace;
- bone is dry/porous and lighter;
- Shroud matter is wet/organic/soft or crusted depending on layer;
- energy is emissive and localized.

Do not make every edge emissive. Light must reveal hierarchy.

## Texture rules

- Vanilla 16×16 is not a mandatory ceiling for hero assets.
- Texel density should be consistent within an asset family.
- Hero textures may use higher resolution when it materially improves carved detail/material readability.
- Avoid noisy detail that disappears at normal play distance.
- Environment families need tiling and mass-placement review.
- Random/connected/continuous enhancement should be used to break repetition where appropriate.

## Emissive rules

Use emissive for:
- inner Flame source;
- Shroud heart/core;
- active rune channels;
- Lich spectral fractures;
- ritual activation state.

Do not use emissive as substitute for good geometry/material design.

## Motion rules

### Flame motion
- stable breathing;
- vertical lift;
- rotational/radial ritual motion;
- progression makes the system feel more complete, not merely faster.

### Shroud motion
- offset organic timing;
- pulse + subtle tendril movement;
- no perfectly synchronized “machine” movement;
- death/collapse should feel like loss of pressure/life.

### Lich motion
- floating offsets;
- delayed fragment follow;
- ritual snap/phase transitions only on important events.

## UI language

HUD should combine:
- restrained carved/ritual frame;
- clear state icons;
- readable reserve/progression values;
- Shroud danger motifs on hostile states;
- Flame/Sanctuary motifs on protection states.

Function remains primary. Ornamental art may not reduce localization room or warning legibility.

## Environmental hierarchy

Every Shroud scene should read at four distances:

1. **far:** fog/color mass and skyline corruption;
2. **mid:** large growth silhouettes / ruins / core landmark;
3. **near:** veins, sludge, membranes, rubble and local particles;
4. **interaction:** hero object material/animation detail.

The scene must still communicate Shroud when enhanced fog is disabled.

## Anti-patterns

Reject:
- hero objects made from random vanilla textures;
- pure color swaps for severity;
- neon everywhere;
- generic purple-magic particle spam;
- perfectly clean symmetric Shroud growth;
- tiny decorative cubes/bones with no visible gameplay-scale value;
- UI ornament that competes with timers/warnings;
- models whose only impressive angle is the Blockbench preview.

## Review requirement

Every hero asset needs in-game screenshots in daylight/night, near/mid distance, active/inactive states and reduced-effects mode before art approval.
